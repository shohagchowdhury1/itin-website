## What Is Included in Every ITIN for H4 Visa Package?

Both the $197 Standard and $297 Express packages include 4 core services. Our Certifying Acceptance Agent handles the entire process.

### Complete W-7 Form Preparation

Form W-7 is the official IRS application for an ITIN. Errors on this form are the #1 reason for ITIN rejections. Our team completes every section based on your situation as an H4 visa holder, whether you are a spouse or child of an H1B worker.

### Document Review Against 13 IRS-Accepted IDs

The IRS accepts 13 identity documents for ITIN applications. H4 visa holders typically use their home country passport and H4 visa. We also accept birth certificates for minors. Before we submit anything, we review every document against current IRS requirements.

### IRS Submission and Free Resubmission

We compile your complete application package and submit it to the IRS. Your W-7 form, supporting documents, and the primary visa holder's tax return are sent together. If the IRS requests corrections, we resubmit at no additional cost.

### No Passport Mailing Required

Working with an IRS-authorized Certifying Acceptance Agent (per IRS Publication 1915) means you do not mail your original passport to the IRS. We review and certify your identity documents. Your passport stays with you.

## ITIN for H4 Visa FAQ (2026)

**Do all H4 visa holders need an ITIN?**
Yes, if they are claimed on a US tax return. Most H4 spouses and children of H1B holders need one.

**Can H4 visa holders work with an ITIN?**
No. An ITIN does not give work authorization. H4 holders with an EAD can work, but they still need an SSN or ITIN for taxes.

**How much does it cost for H4 visa holders?**
Standard is $197 with 7-10 business day preparation. Express is $297 with 2-3 business day preparation.

**What documents do H4 visa holders need?**
Your passport, H4 visa, and proof of relationship to the H1B holder. Children need a birth certificate.

**Can I apply for an ITIN while in H4 status?**
Yes. You can apply from inside the US using our CAA service.
