## What Is Included in Every ITIN for F1 Visa Package?

Both the $197 Standard and $297 Express packages include 4 core services. Our Certifying Acceptance Agent handles the entire process.

### Complete W-7 Form Preparation

Form W-7 is the official IRS application for an ITIN. Errors on this form are the #1 reason for ITIN rejections. Our team completes every section based on your situation as an F1 visa holder, whether you have scholarship income, on-campus employment, or OPT authorization.

### Document Review Against 13 IRS-Accepted IDs

The IRS accepts 13 identity documents for ITIN applications. F1 visa holders typically use their home country passport and F1 visa. We also accept Form I-20 as supporting documentation. Before we submit anything, we review every document against current IRS requirements.

### IRS Submission and Free Resubmission

We compile your complete application package and submit it to the IRS. Your W-7 form, supporting documents, and tax return are sent together. If the IRS requests corrections, we resubmit at no additional cost.

### No Passport Mailing Required

Working with an IRS-authorized Certifying Acceptance Agent (per IRS Publication 1915) means you do not mail your original passport to the IRS. We review and certify your identity documents. Your passport stays with you.

## ITIN for F1 Visa FAQ (2026)

**Do F1 visa holders need an ITIN?**
Yes, if you have US income such as scholarships, fellowships, or on-campus wages and cannot get an SSN.

**Can F1 students get an SSN instead of an ITIN?**
F1 students with on-campus work authorization or CPT/OPT can apply for an SSN. If you do not have work authorization, you need an ITIN.

**How much does it cost for F1 visa holders?**
Standard is $197 with 7-10 business day preparation. Express is $297 with 2-3 business day preparation.

**What documents do F1 visa holders need?**
Your passport, F1 visa, and Form I-20. Some students also need a tax return or exception documentation.

**Do F1 students need to file a tax return?**
Yes. Most F1 students must file Form 8843. If you have income, you also file Form 1040-NR.
