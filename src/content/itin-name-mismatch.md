## ITIN Name Mismatch FAQ (2026)

**Why do name mismatches happen?**
Different spellings on passport, W-7, and tax return. Middle name omissions also cause issues.

**Can a name mismatch cause rejection?**
Yes. The IRS rejects applications when names do not match official documents.

**How much does it cost to fix a name mismatch?**
Standard is $197 with 7-10 business day preparation. Express is $297 with 2-3 business day preparation.

**What if my passport has my maiden name?**
Use the name on your passport consistently across all forms. Update other documents if needed.

**Can I fix a name mismatch on an existing ITIN?**
Yes. Submit Form W-7 with the correct name and supporting documents.
