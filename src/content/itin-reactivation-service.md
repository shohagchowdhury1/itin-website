## What Is Included in Every ITIN Re-activation Service Package?

Both the $197 Standard and $297 Express packages include 4 core services. The IRS requires Form W-7 for all ITIN re-activations. Our Certifying Acceptance Agent handles the entire process.

Form W-7 is the official IRS form for ITIN re-activation. Errors on this form are the #1 reason for rejection. Our team completes every section based on your situation, whether your ITIN expired from non-use or needs updating.

### Document Review Against 13 IRS-Accepted IDs

The IRS accepts 13 identity documents for ITIN re-activations. Before we submit anything, we review every document against current IRS requirements. If something is missing, we tell you before it becomes a rejection.

### IRS Submission and Free Resubmission

We compile your complete re-activation package and submit it to the IRS. Your W-7 form and supporting documents are sent together. If the IRS requests corrections, we resubmit at no additional cost.

### No Passport Mailing Required

Working with an IRS-authorized Certifying Acceptance Agent (per IRS Publication 1915) means you do not mail your original passport to the IRS. We review and certify your identity documents. Your passport stays with you.

## ITIN Re-activation Service FAQ (2026)

**What is ITIN re-activation?**
Re-activation is for ITINs that expired because they were not used on a tax return for 3 consecutive years. You must submit a new Form W-7 to re-activate.

**Is re-activation different from renewal?**
Yes. Renewal is for ITINs with expired middle digits. Re-activation is for ITINs that expired from non-use. Both use Form W-7.

**How much does ITIN re-activation cost?**
Standard is $197 with 7-10 business day preparation. Express is $297 with 2-3 business day preparation.

**Do I need to send my passport again?**
Yes. You must provide current identity documents even if you submitted them before.

**Can I file taxes while waiting for re-activation?**
You can file, but the IRS may process your return without the ITIN. It is better to re-activate first, then file.
