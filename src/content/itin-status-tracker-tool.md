We provide regular updates on your application status. We also guide you on how to check directly with the IRS if needed.

We compile your complete application package and submit it to the IRS. Your W-7 form, supporting documents, and tax return are sent together. If the IRS requests corrections, we resubmit at no additional cost.

### No Passport Mailing Required

Working with an IRS-authorized Certifying Acceptance Agent (per IRS Publication 1915) means you do not mail your original passport to the IRS. We review and certify your identity documents. Your passport stays with you.

## ITIN Status Tracker Tool FAQ (2026)

**How can I track my ITIN application?**
Call the IRS at 1-800-829-1040 or 1-267-941-1000. Have your name and mailing address ready.

**Can I track my ITIN online?**
The IRS does not offer online tracking for ITINs. Phone is the only option.

**How much does status tracking cost?**
Standard is $197 with 7-10 business day preparation. Express is $297 with 2-3 business day preparation.

**How often should I check my status?**
Wait at least 6 weeks before calling. Calling too early will not speed up processing.

**What if my status shows no record?**
It may mean your application was not yet entered. Wait another 2 weeks and call again.
