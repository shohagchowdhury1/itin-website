## What Is Included in Every ITIN for S Corp Package?

Both the $197 Standard and $297 Express packages include 4 core services. Our Certifying Acceptance Agent handles the entire process.

### Complete W-7 Form Preparation

Form W-7 is the official IRS application for an ITIN. Errors on this form are the #1 reason for ITIN rejections. Our team completes every section based on your situation as an S Corp shareholder, whether you are an active owner or passive investor.

### Document Review Against 13 IRS-Accepted IDs

The IRS accepts 13 identity documents for ITIN applications. S Corp shareholders typically use their home country passport. Before we submit anything, we review every document against current IRS requirements.

### IRS Submission and Free Resubmission

We compile your complete application package and submit it to the IRS. Your W-7 form, supporting documents, and tax return are sent together. If the IRS requests corrections, we resubmit at no additional cost.

### No Passport Mailing Required

Working with an IRS-authorized Certifying Acceptance Agent (per IRS Publication 1915) means you do not mail your original passport to the IRS. We review and certify your identity documents. Your passport stays with you.

## ITIN for S Corp FAQ (2026)

**Can non-US residents own an S Corp?**
No. S Corps cannot have non-resident alien shareholders. Only US citizens and residents can own S Corp shares.

**Do S Corp shareholders need an ITIN?**
If you are a resident alien shareholder, you may need an ITIN if you cannot get an SSN.

**How much does it cost for S Corp shareholders?**
Standard is $197 with 7-10 business day preparation. Express is $297 with 2-3 business day preparation.

**What tax forms do S Corp shareholders file?**
Shareholders file Form 1040 or 1040-NR and receive Schedule K-1 from the S Corp.

**Can an ITIN holder be an S Corp shareholder?**
Only if the ITIN holder is a US resident. Non-residents cannot own S Corp shares.
