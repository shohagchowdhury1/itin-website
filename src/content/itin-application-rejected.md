## ITIN Application Rejected FAQ (2026)

**Why was my ITIN application rejected?**
Common reasons: wrong reason code, name mismatch, unsigned form, expired passport, missing tax return, or notarized copies.

**Can I fix a rejected ITIN application?**
Yes. We analyze the rejection, fix the errors, and resubmit at no extra cost.

**How much does it cost to fix a rejected application?**
Standard is $197 with 7-10 business day preparation. Express is $297 with 2-3 business day preparation.

**How long does it take to fix a rejection?**
Preparation takes 2-10 business days. IRS processing takes another 6-11 weeks.

**Will I get a new ITIN number after rejection?**
If this was your first application, you will receive a new ITIN. If you already had one, we renew your existing number.
