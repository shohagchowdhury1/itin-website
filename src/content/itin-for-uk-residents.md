## What Is Included in Every ITIN for UK Residents Package?

Both the $197 Standard and $297 Express packages include 4 core services. Our Certifying Acceptance Agent handles the entire process remotely from the UK.

### Complete W-7 Form Preparation

Form W-7 is the official IRS application for an ITIN. Errors on this form are the #1 reason for ITIN rejections. Our team completes every section based on your situation as a UK resident, whether you are an investor, remote worker, or business owner.

### Document Review Against 13 IRS-Accepted IDs

The IRS accepts 13 identity documents for ITIN applications. Most UK residents use their UK passport. Before we submit anything, we review every document against current IRS requirements.

### IRS Submission and Free Resubmission

We compile your complete application package and submit it to the IRS. Your W-7 form, supporting documents, and tax return are sent together. If the IRS requests corrections, we resubmit at no additional cost.

### No Passport Mailing Required

Working with an IRS-authorized Certifying Acceptance Agent (per IRS Publication 1915) means you do not mail your original UK passport to the IRS. We review and certify your identity documents. Your passport stays with you in the UK.

## ITIN for UK Residents FAQ (2026)

**Can I apply for an ITIN from the UK?**
Yes. Our CAA service is fully remote. You never need to visit the US.

**What documents do UK residents need?**
A valid UK passport is the best and only document most UK residents need.

**How much does it cost for UK residents?**
Standard is $197 with 7-10 business day preparation. Express is $297 with 2-3 business day preparation.

**Can UK residents claim tax treaty benefits?**
Yes. The UK has a tax treaty with the US. An ITIN lets you claim these benefits on your tax return.

**Do I need a US tax return to apply?**
Yes, unless you qualify for one of 5 IRS exceptions. Most UK residents attach Form 1040-NR.
