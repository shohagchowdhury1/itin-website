## What Is Included in Every ITIN Without Tax Return Package?

Both the $197 Standard and $297 Express packages include 4 core services. Our Certifying Acceptance Agent handles the entire process.

### Complete W-7 Form Preparation Under Exception

Form W-7 is the official IRS application for an ITIN. Errors on this form are the #1 reason for ITIN rejections. Our team completes every section and selects the correct exception category so you do not need to attach a tax return.

### Document Review Against 13 IRS-Accepted IDs

The IRS accepts 13 identity documents for ITIN applications. Applicants typically use their home country passport. Before we submit anything, we review every document against current IRS requirements.

### IRS Submission and Free Resubmission

We compile your complete application package and submit it to the IRS. Your W-7 form, supporting documents, and exception documentation are sent together. If the IRS requests corrections, we resubmit at no additional cost.

### No Passport Mailing Required

Working with an IRS-authorized Certifying Acceptance Agent (per IRS Publication 1915) means you do not mail your original passport to the IRS. We review and certify your identity documents. Your passport stays with you.

## ITIN Without Tax Return FAQ (2026)

**Can I get an ITIN without filing a tax return?**
Yes, if you qualify for one of 5 IRS exceptions.

**What are the 5 exceptions?**
1. Passive income from US sources
2. Tax treaty benefits
3. Mortgage interest refund
4. Third-party withholding on passive income
5. IRS reporting requirements for partners or LLC members

**How much does it cost without a tax return?**
Standard is $197 with 7-10 business day preparation. Express is $297 with 2-3 business day preparation.

**Do I need a letter from a bank or third party?**
Some exceptions require supporting letters. We help you obtain the right documentation.

**Is it harder to get an ITIN without a tax return?**
Not if you qualify for an exception. We prepare the correct paperwork to prove your eligibility.
