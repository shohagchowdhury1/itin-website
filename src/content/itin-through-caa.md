## What Is Included in Every ITIN Through CAA Package?

Both the $197 Standard and $297 Express packages include 4 core services. Our Certifying Acceptance Agent handles the entire process.

### Complete W-7 Form Preparation

Form W-7 is the official IRS application for an ITIN. Errors on this form are the #1 reason for ITIN rejections. Our team completes every section based on your situation.

### CAA Document Certification

As an IRS-authorized Certifying Acceptance Agent, we certify your identity documents per IRS Publication 1915. This means you never mail your original passport to the IRS.

### IRS Submission and Free Resubmission

We compile your complete application package and submit it to the IRS. Your W-7 form, certified documents, and tax return are sent together. If the IRS requests corrections, we resubmit at no additional cost.

### No Passport Mailing Required

Working with a CAA means you do not mail your original passport to the IRS. We review and certify your identity documents. Your passport stays with you.

## ITIN Through CAA FAQ (2026)

**What is a CAA?**
A Certifying Acceptance Agent is authorized by the IRS to verify identity documents and submit ITIN applications.

**Why use a CAA instead of applying directly?**
A CAA certifies your documents so you keep your passport. CAAs also review applications for errors before submission.

**How much does CAA service cost?**
Standard is $197 with 7-10 business day preparation. Express is $297 with 2-3 business day preparation.

**Can I apply through a CAA from any country?**
Yes. Our CAA service works remotely with clients in 150+ countries.

**Is CAA certification the same as notarization?**
No. CAA certification is IRS-specific and accepted by the IRS. Notarization is not accepted.
