## What Is Included in Every ITIN Status Check Service Package?

Both the $197 Standard and $297 Express packages include 4 core services. Our Certifying Acceptance Agent handles the entire status check process.

### Complete Status Inquiry Preparation

We prepare a formal status inquiry for the IRS. This includes your name, mailing address, date of birth, and any prior correspondence. Errors in status requests are a common reason for delayed responses.

### Document Review If Needed

If the IRS needs updated documents to locate your application, we review them against the 13 IRS-accepted IDs before submission. If something is missing, we tell you before it becomes a problem.

### IRS Follow-Up and Free Re-Check

We submit your status inquiry to the IRS and follow up if there is no response. If the first inquiry does not yield results, we re-check at no additional cost.

### No Passport Mailing Required

If updated documents are needed, working with an IRS-authorized Certifying Acceptance Agent (per IRS Publication 1915) means you do not mail your original passport to the IRS. We review and certify your identity documents. Your passport stays with you.

## ITIN Status Check Service FAQ (2026)

**How do I check my ITIN status?**
You can call the IRS at 1-800-829-1040 or use our status check service. We handle the call and paperwork for you.

**What information do I need to check my ITIN status?**
You need your full name, mailing address, date of birth, and the date you applied.

**How much does ITIN status check cost?**
Standard is $197 with 7-10 business day preparation. Express is $297 with 2-3 business day preparation.

**What if the IRS has no record of my application?**
We investigate further. It may mean your application was lost or delayed. We help you resubmit if needed.

**Can I check status for a rejected application?**
Yes. We can check why it was rejected and help you fix the issues.
