Both the $197 Standard and $297 Express packages include 4 core services. Our Certifying Acceptance Agent handles the entire process.

### Complete W-7 Form Preparation

Form W-7 is the official IRS application for an ITIN. Errors on this form are the #1 reason for ITIN rejections. Our team completes every section based on your certified copy documentation.

### Certified Copy Verification

The IRS accepts certified copies from the issuing government agency. We verify that your copies are properly certified and meet all IRS requirements before submission.

### IRS Submission and Free Resubmission

We compile your complete application package and submit it to the IRS. Your W-7 form, certified copies, and tax return are sent together. If the IRS requests corrections, we resubmit at no additional cost.

### No Original Document Mailing Required

Working with an IRS-authorized Certifying Acceptance Agent (per IRS Publication 1915) means you do not mail your original documents to the IRS. We review and certify your copies.

## ITIN With Certified Copies FAQ (2026)

**What is a certified copy?**
A copy certified by the issuing government agency as a true copy of the original document.

**Can I use a notarized copy?**
No. The IRS does not accept notarized copies. Only certified copies from the issuing agency are accepted.

**How much does it cost with certified copies?**
Standard is $197 with 7-10 business day preparation. Express is $297 with 2-3 business day preparation.

**How do I get a certified copy?**
Contact the government agency that issued your original document. They will provide an official certified copy for a fee.

**Can a CAA certify my documents instead?**
Yes. A Certifying Acceptance Agent can certify your original passport, so you never need certified copies.
