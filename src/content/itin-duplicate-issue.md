## ITIN Duplicate Issue FAQ (2026)

**Can I have more than one ITIN?**
No. The IRS only allows one ITIN per person. Duplicates must be resolved.

**How do duplicates happen?**
Applying multiple times, name variations, or IRS data entry errors.

**How much does duplicate resolution cost?**
Standard is $197 with 7-10 business day preparation. Express is $297 with 2-3 business day preparation.

**Which ITIN will the IRS keep?**
Usually the first one issued. We help you keep the correct number.

**Will my tax history transfer to the correct ITIN?**
Yes. The IRS merges tax records under the retained ITIN.
