## What Is Included in Every ITIN With Tax Return Package?

Both the $197 Standard and $297 Express packages include 4 core services. Our Certifying Acceptance Agent handles the entire process.

### Complete W-7 Form Preparation

Form W-7 is the official IRS application for an ITIN. Errors on this form are the #1 reason for ITIN rejections. Our team completes every section based on your tax situation, whether you file Form 1040-NR or another return.

### Tax Return Preparation Guidance

We guide you on how to prepare your tax return to attach to Form W-7. Most applicants file Form 1040-NR. We tell you which schedules and forms you need based on your income sources.

### IRS Submission and Free Resubmission

We compile your complete application package and submit it to the IRS. Your W-7 form, tax return, and supporting documents are sent together. If the IRS requests corrections, we resubmit at no additional cost.

### No Passport Mailing Required

Working with an IRS-authorized Certifying Acceptance Agent (per IRS Publication 1915) means you do not mail your original passport to the IRS. We review and certify your identity documents. Your passport stays with you.

## ITIN With Tax Return FAQ (2026)

**Do I need a tax return to get an ITIN?**
Yes, unless you qualify for one of 5 IRS exceptions. Most applicants attach a tax return.

**Which tax return do I attach?**
Most non-residents attach Form 1040-NR. US residents attach Form 1040.

**How much does it cost with a tax return?**
Standard is $197 with 7-10 business day preparation. Express is $297 with 2-3 business day preparation.

**Can I file my tax return before getting an ITIN?**
Yes. You write "ITIN TO BE REQUESTED" where the ITIN number goes.

**Will the IRS process my tax return with my ITIN application?**
Yes. The IRS processes both together and assigns your ITIN to your tax record.
