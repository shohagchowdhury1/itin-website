## What Is Included in Every ITIN Emergency Processing Package?

Both the $197 Standard and $297 Express packages include 4 core services. Our Certifying Acceptance Agent handles the entire process.

### Complete W-7 Form Preparation

Form W-7 is the official IRS application for an ITIN. Errors on this form are the #1 reason for ITIN rejections. Our team completes every section based on your emergency situation.

### Document Review Against 13 IRS-Accepted IDs

The IRS accepts 13 identity documents for ITIN applications. Emergency applicants typically use their home country passport. Before we submit anything, we review every document against current IRS requirements.

### Urgent IRS Submission and Free Resubmission

We compile your complete application package and submit it to the IRS immediately. Your W-7 form, supporting documents, and tax return are sent together. If the IRS requests corrections, we resubmit at no additional cost.

### No Passport Mailing Required

Working with an IRS-authorized Certifying Acceptance Agent (per IRS Publication 1915) means you do not mail your original passport to the IRS. We review and certify your identity documents. Your passport stays with you.

## ITIN Emergency Processing FAQ (2026)

**Is there an IRS emergency processing option?**
No. The IRS does not offer emergency or expedited ITIN processing.

**How fast is emergency processing?**
Express preparation takes 2-3 business days. Total time is 7-12 weeks including IRS processing.

**How much does emergency processing cost?**
Standard is $197 with 7-10 business day preparation. Express is $297 with 2-3 business day preparation.

**What counts as an emergency?**
Upcoming tax deadline, business closing, or account suspension are common emergencies.

**Can I pay extra for faster IRS processing?**
No. The IRS does not accept payment for faster processing.
