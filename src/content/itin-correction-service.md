## What Is Included in Every ITIN Correction Service Package?

Both the $197 Standard and $297 Express packages include 4 core services. The IRS requires Form W-7 for all ITIN corrections. Our Certifying Acceptance Agent handles the entire process.

### Complete W-7 Form Preparation

Form W-7 is the official IRS form for ITIN correction. Errors on this form are the #1 reason for rejection. Our team completes every section correctly to fix your name, date of birth, or other details.

### Document Review Against 13 IRS-Accepted IDs

The IRS accepts 13 identity documents for ITIN corrections. Before we submit anything, we review every document against current IRS requirements. If something is missing, we tell you before it becomes a rejection.

### IRS Submission and Free Resubmission

We compile your complete correction package and submit it to the IRS. Your W-7 form and supporting documents are sent together. If the IRS requests more corrections, we resubmit at no additional cost.

### No Passport Mailing Required

Working with an IRS-authorized Certifying Acceptance Agent (per IRS Publication 1915) means you do not mail your original passport to the IRS. We review and certify your identity documents. Your passport stays with you.

## ITIN Correction Service FAQ (2026)

**What can be corrected on an ITIN?**
You can correct your name, date of birth, country of citizenship, or other personal details. You cannot change the ITIN number itself.

**How do I know if my ITIN has an error?**
Check your CP565 notice or IRS letters. If your name is misspelled or your date of birth is wrong, you need a correction.

**How much does ITIN correction cost?**
Standard is $197 with 7-10 business day preparation. Express is $297 with 2-3 business day preparation.

**Do I need to send my passport for a correction?**
Yes. You must provide current identity documents that show the correct information.

**Will my ITIN number change after correction?**
No. Your ITIN number stays the same. Only the personal information linked to it is updated.
