If your ITIN is expired or expiring, we prepare Form W-7 for renewal. Errors on this form are the #1 reason for rejection. Our team completes every section correctly.

We compile your complete renewal package and submit it to the IRS. Your W-7 form and supporting documents are sent together. If the IRS requests corrections, we resubmit at no additional cost.

## ITIN Expiration Checker FAQ (2026)

**How do I know if my ITIN expired?**
ITINs with middle digits 70-88 expired. Also, unused ITINs expire after 3 years.

**Can I check expiration for free?**
Yes. Contact us with your ITIN number for a free expiration check.

**How much does the expiration checker cost?**
Standard is $197 with 7-10 business day preparation. Express is $297 with 2-3 business day preparation.

**What happens if my ITIN expired?**
You must submit Form W-7 to renew it before filing taxes or using it for banking.

**Can I prevent my ITIN from expiring?**
Yes. Use your ITIN on a federal tax return at least once every 3 years.
