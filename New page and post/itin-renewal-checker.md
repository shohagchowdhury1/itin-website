## What Is Included in Every ITIN Renewal Checker Package?

Both the $197 Standard and $297 Express packages include 4 core services. Our Certifying Acceptance Agent handles the entire process.

### Renewal Eligibility Check

We check if your ITIN expired. ITINs with middle digits 70-88 expired. ITINs not used on a tax return for 3 consecutive years also expire.

### Complete W-7 Form Preparation

Form W-7 is the official IRS form for ITIN renewal. Errors on this form are the #1 reason for rejection. Our team completes every section correctly.

### IRS Submission and Free Resubmission

We compile your complete renewal package and submit it to the IRS. Your W-7 form and supporting documents are sent together. If the IRS requests corrections, we resubmit at no additional cost.

### No Passport Mailing Required

Working with an IRS-authorized Certifying Acceptance Agent (per IRS Publication 1915) means you do not mail your original passport to the IRS. We review and certify your identity documents. Your passport stays with you.

Check the middle digits. 70-88 expired. Also, if unused for 3 years, it expires.

**How much does the renewal checker cost?**

**What if my ITIN is still active?**

**Do I need a tax return to renew?**
No. Renewal does not require a tax return unless you are also filing taxes.
