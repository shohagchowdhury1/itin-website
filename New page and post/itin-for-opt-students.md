## What Is Included in Every ITIN for OPT Students Package?

Both the $197 Standard and $297 Express packages include 4 core services. Our Certifying Acceptance Agent handles the entire process.

### Complete W-7 Form Preparation

Form W-7 is the official IRS application for an ITIN. Errors on this form are the #1 reason for ITIN rejections. Our team completes every section based on your situation as an OPT student, whether you are working for a US employer or doing freelance work.

### Document Review Against 13 IRS-Accepted IDs

The IRS accepts 13 identity documents for ITIN applications. OPT students typically use their home country passport and EAD card. We also accept Form I-20 as supporting documentation. Before we submit anything, we review every document against current IRS requirements.

### IRS Submission and Free Resubmission

We compile your complete application package and submit it to the IRS. Your W-7 form, supporting documents, and tax return are sent together. If the IRS requests corrections, we resubmit at no additional cost.

### No Passport Mailing Required

Working with an IRS-authorized Certifying Acceptance Agent (per IRS Publication 1915) means you do not mail your original passport to the IRS. We review and certify your identity documents. Your passport stays with you.

## ITIN for OPT Students FAQ (2026)

**Do OPT students need an ITIN?**
Yes, if you have US income and cannot get an SSN. Many OPT students qualify for an SSN through employment.

**Can OPT students get an SSN instead of an ITIN?**
If you have a job offer and an EAD card, you can apply for an SSN. If not, you need an ITIN.

**How much does it cost for OPT students?**
Standard is $197 with 7-10 business day preparation. Express is $297 with 2-3 business day preparation.

**What documents do OPT students need?**
Your passport, EAD card, and Form I-20. Some students also need a job offer letter or tax return.

**Do OPT students pay US taxes?**
Yes. OPT income is taxable. You file Form 1040-NR and may need an ITIN to do so.
