## What Is Included in Every ITIN for Business Banking Package?

Both the $197 Standard and $297 Express packages include 4 core services. Our Certifying Acceptance Agent handles the entire process.

### Complete W-7 Form Preparation

Form W-7 is the official IRS application for an ITIN. Errors on this form are the #1 reason for ITIN rejections. Our team completes every section based on your situation as a business owner needing banking services.

### Document Review Against 13 IRS-Accepted IDs

The IRS accepts 13 identity documents for ITIN applications. Business banking applicants typically use their home country passport. Before we submit anything, we review every document against current IRS requirements.

### IRS Submission and Free Resubmission

We compile your complete application package and submit it to the IRS. Your W-7 form, supporting documents, and tax return are sent together. If the IRS requests corrections, we resubmit at no additional cost.

### No Passport Mailing Required

Working with an IRS-authorized Certifying Acceptance Agent (per IRS Publication 1915) means you do not mail your original passport to the IRS. We review and certify your identity documents. Your passport stays with you.

## ITIN for Business Banking FAQ (2026)

**Can I open a business bank account with an ITIN?**
Yes. Many US banks accept an ITIN for business account ownership verification.

**Do I need an EIN too for business banking?**
Yes. Most banks require both an EIN for the business and an ITIN for the owner.

**How much does it cost to get an ITIN for business banking?**
Standard is $197 with 7-10 business day preparation. Express is $297 with 2-3 business day preparation.

**Which banks accept ITIN for business accounts?**
Mercury, Relay, and some regional banks accept ITINs. Major banks often require an SSN.

**Can I get a business debit card with an ITIN?**
Yes. Most business banks that accept ITINs issue debit cards.
