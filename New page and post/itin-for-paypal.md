## What Is Included in Every ITIN for PayPal Package?

Both the $197 Standard and $297 Express packages include 4 core services. Our Certifying Acceptance Agent handles the entire process.

### Complete W-7 Form Preparation

Form W-7 is the official IRS application for an ITIN. Errors on this form are the #1 reason for ITIN rejections. Our team completes every section based on your situation as a PayPal user, whether you sell products, offer services, or receive payments globally.

### Document Review Against 13 IRS-Accepted IDs

The IRS accepts 13 identity documents for ITIN applications. PayPal users typically use their home country passport. Before we submit anything, we review every document against current IRS requirements.

### IRS Submission and Free Resubmission

We compile your complete application package and submit it to the IRS. Your W-7 form, supporting documents, and tax return are sent together. If the IRS requests corrections, we resubmit at no additional cost.

### No Passport Mailing Required

Working with an IRS-authorized Certifying Acceptance Agent (per IRS Publication 1915) means you do not mail your original passport to the IRS. We review and certify your identity documents. Your passport stays with you.

## ITIN for PayPal FAQ (2026)

**Do I need an ITIN for PayPal?**
PayPal may request a tax ID for US accounts or high-volume sellers. An ITIN satisfies this requirement.

**Can I use PayPal without an ITIN?**
Yes for personal accounts. Business accounts and high-volume sellers may need a tax ID.

**How much does it cost to get an ITIN for PayPal?**
Standard is $197 with 7-10 business day preparation. Express is $297 with 2-3 business day preparation.

**Does PayPal report to the IRS?**
Yes. PayPal files 1099-K for sellers over certain thresholds. You need an ITIN to match these reports.

**Can I link PayPal to my US bank account with an ITIN?**
Yes. An ITIN helps verify your identity for US financial services.
