## What Do You Need to Apply for an ITIN?

You need 3 things to apply: a completed IRS Form W-7, valid identity and foreign status documents, and a federal tax return unless you qualify for an exception.

### ITIN Application Checklist

- **IRS Form W-7**: 2-page form every applicant must complete and sign
- **Identity documents**: the IRS accepts 13 types. A valid passport is the single document that proves both identity and foreign status
- **Federal tax return**: Form 1040 or 1040-NR, attached unless you qualify for an exception
- **Reason for applying**: select the correct category on W-7 line 6d

## Step 1: Are You Eligible for an ITIN?

You are eligible for an ITIN if you have a US federal tax filing obligation and you are not eligible for a Social Security Number. The IRS lists 6 categories on Form W-7 line 6d:

- **Non-resident aliens filing a US tax return**
- **Spouses or dependents of US citizens/residents**
- **Non-resident aliens claiming a tax treaty benefit**
- **Foreign nationals with US-source income subject to withholding**
- **Non-resident members of a US partnership**
- **Foreign LLC owners**

If you already have an SSN, the IRS will reject your ITIN application.

## Step 2: Which 13 Documents Does the IRS Accept?

The IRS accepts 13 types of identity documents. All must be originals or certified copies. Notarized copies are not accepted.

### Complete List of 13 Accepted Documents

01. Passport (standalone - proves both identity and foreign status)
02. National identification card
03. US driver's license
04. Foreign driver's license
05. Birth certificate (for dependents under 18)
06. Foreign voter's registration card
07. US military identification card
08. Foreign military identification card
09. US state identification card
10. Civil birth certificate
11. Medical records (dependents under 6 only)
12. School records (dependents under 18 only)
13. US visa issued by the Department of State

### Document Rules to Know

- All documents must be current and unexpired
- Without a passport, you need at least 2 documents
- Certified copies must come from the issuing government agency
- A CAA can certify your passport per IRS Publication 1915 so you never mail the original

## Step 3: How Do You Fill Out IRS Form W-7?

Form W-7 is 2 pages. Errors are the #1 cause of rejections.

### 8 Critical Form W-7 Fields

- **Line 1a-1b:** Legal name exactly as on passport
- **Line 2:** Foreign mailing address
- **Line 3:** US address, if applicable
- **Line 4:** Date of birth (MM/DD/YYYY)
- **Line 5:** Country of citizenship
- **Line 6a:** Foreign tax identification number
- **Line 6d:** Reason for applying (most critical field)
- **Signature:** Must sign and date

## What Are the 8 Most Common ITIN Rejection Reasons?

1. Missing or incorrect information on Form W-7
2. No federal tax return attached
3. Unsigned application
4. Expired passport or identity documents
5. Wrong reason code on line 6d
6. Notarized copies instead of certified copies
7. SSN eligibility
8. Duplicate application
