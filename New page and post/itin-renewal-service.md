## What Is Included in Every ITIN Renewal Service Package?

Both the $197 Standard and $297 Express packages include 4 core services. The IRS requires Form W-7 for all ITIN renewals. Our Certifying Acceptance Agent handles the entire process.

### Complete W-7 Form Preparation

Form W-7 is the official IRS form for ITIN renewal. Errors on this form are the #1 reason for rejection. Our team completes every section based on your situation, whether your ITIN expired or you need to renew for a dependent.

### Document Review Against 13 IRS-Accepted IDs

The IRS accepts 13 identity documents for ITIN renewals. Before we submit anything, we review every document against current IRS requirements. If something is missing, we tell you before it becomes a rejection.

### IRS Submission and Free Resubmission

We compile your complete renewal package and submit it to the IRS. Your W-7 form and supporting documents are sent together. If the IRS requests corrections, we resubmit at no additional cost.

### No Passport Mailing Required

Working with an IRS-authorized Certifying Acceptance Agent (per IRS Publication 1915) means you do not mail your original passport to the IRS. We review and certify your identity documents. Your passport stays with you.

## ITIN Renewal Service FAQ (2026)

**When do I need to renew my ITIN?**
ITINs with middle digits 70-88 expired and must be renewed. ITINs not used on a tax return for 3 consecutive years also expire.

**Can I renew an expired ITIN?**
Yes. You submit a new Form W-7 with your expired ITIN number and current identity documents.

**How much does ITIN renewal cost?**
Standard is $197 with 7-10 business day preparation. Express is $297 with 2-3 business day preparation.

**Do I need a tax return to renew my ITIN?**
No. Renewal does not require a tax return unless you are also using the ITIN to file taxes.

**What happens if I file taxes with an expired ITIN?**
The IRS will process your return but may delay your refund. Renew before filing to avoid delays.
