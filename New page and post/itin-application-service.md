## What Is Included in Every ITIN Application Service Package?

Both the $197 Standard and $297 Express packages include 4 core services. The IRS requires Form W-7 (per IRC Section 6109) for all ITIN applications. Our Certifying Acceptance Agent handles the entire process.

### Complete W-7 Form Preparation

Form W-7 is the official IRS application for an ITIN. Errors on this form are the #1 reason for ITIN rejections. Our team completes every section of the W-7 based on your specific situation, whether you are an Amazon seller, freelancer, real estate investor, or filing a US tax return for another reason.

### Document Review Against 13 IRS-Accepted IDs

The IRS accepts 13 identity documents for ITIN applications. Before we submit anything to the IRS, we review every document you provide against current IRS requirements. If something is missing, we tell you before it becomes a rejection.

### IRS Submission and Free Resubmission

We compile your complete application package and submit it to the IRS. Your W-7 form, supporting documents, and tax return are sent together. If the IRS requests corrections, we resubmit at no additional cost. Check your [application status](/itin-status-check-service) anytime during processing.

### No Passport Mailing Required

Working with an IRS-authorized Certifying Acceptance Agent (per IRS Publication 1915) means you do not mail your original passport to the IRS. We review and certify your identity documents. Your passport stays with you. This is a key advantage over applying directly through the IRS.

## ITIN Application Service FAQ (2026)

**What is an ITIN application service?**
An ITIN application service prepares your Form W-7, reviews your documents, and submits everything to the IRS. At itin.so, our CAA team handles the full process so you avoid common mistakes.

**How much does the ITIN application service cost?**
Standard is $197 with 7-10 business day preparation. Express is $297 with 2-3 business day preparation. Both include complete W-7 form preparation, document review, and IRS submission.

**Do I need a tax return to apply for an ITIN?**
Yes, unless you qualify for one of 5 IRS exceptions. Most applicants attach Form 1040 or 1040-NR to their W-7.

**Can I apply for an ITIN without a passport?**
Yes, but you need at least 2 other documents from the IRS list of 13 accepted IDs. A passport is the easiest option because it proves both identity and foreign status in one document.

**What happens if my ITIN application is rejected?**
We fix the issue and resubmit at no extra cost. Our Express package includes a 100% money-back guarantee if the IRS rejects due to our preparation error.
