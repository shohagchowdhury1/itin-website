## What Is Included in Every ITIN for Rental Income Package?

Both the $197 Standard and $297 Express packages include 4 core services. Our Certifying Acceptance Agent handles the entire process.

### Complete W-7 Form Preparation

Form W-7 is the official IRS application for an ITIN. Errors on this form are the #1 reason for ITIN rejections. Our team completes every section based on your situation as someone earning US rental income.

### Document Review Against 13 IRS-Accepted IDs

The IRS accepts 13 identity documents for ITIN applications. Rental income earners typically use their home country passport. Before we submit anything, we review every document against current IRS requirements.

### IRS Submission and Free Resubmission

We compile your complete application package and submit it to the IRS. Your W-7 form, supporting documents, and tax return are sent together. If the IRS requests corrections, we resubmit at no additional cost.

### No Passport Mailing Required

Working with an IRS-authorized Certifying Acceptance Agent (per IRS Publication 1915) means you do not mail your original passport to the IRS. We review and certify your identity documents. Your passport stays with you.

## ITIN for Rental Income FAQ (2026)

**Do I need an ITIN for US rental income?**
Yes. The IRS requires all rental income to be reported using a taxpayer identification number.

**What if my property manager withholds 30%?**
With an ITIN, you can file a tax return and claim deductions to reduce or eliminate withholding.

**How much does it cost for rental income ITIN?**
Standard is $197 with 7-10 business day preparation. Express is $297 with 2-3 business day preparation.

**What deductions can I claim on rental income?**
You can deduct property management fees, repairs, insurance, property taxes, and depreciation.

**Do I need an ITIN to sell my rental property?**
Yes. The buyer or escrow company will require a tax ID for withholding and reporting.
