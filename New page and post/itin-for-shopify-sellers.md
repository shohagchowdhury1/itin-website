## What Is Included in Every ITIN for Shopify Sellers Package?

Both the $197 Standard and $297 Express packages include 4 core services. Our Certifying Acceptance Agent handles the entire process.

### Complete W-7 Form Preparation

Form W-7 is the official IRS application for an ITIN. Errors on this form are the #1 reason for ITIN rejections. Our team completes every section based on your situation as a Shopify seller, whether you run a dropshipping store or hold your own inventory.

### Document Review Against 13 IRS-Accepted IDs

The IRS accepts 13 identity documents for ITIN applications. Shopify sellers typically use their home country passport. Before we submit anything, we review every document against current IRS requirements.

### IRS Submission and Free Resubmission

We compile your complete application package and submit it to the IRS. Your W-7 form, supporting documents, and tax return are sent together. If the IRS requests corrections, we resubmit at no additional cost.

### No Passport Mailing Required

Working with an IRS-authorized Certifying Acceptance Agent (per IRS Publication 1915) means you do not mail your original passport to the IRS. We review and certify your identity documents. Your passport stays with you.

## ITIN for Shopify Sellers FAQ (2026)

**Do Shopify sellers need an ITIN?**
Yes, if you have US customers and need to report income to the IRS. Payment processors like Stripe and PayPal also require a tax ID.

**Can I use Stripe with an ITIN?**
Yes. Stripe accepts ITINs for non-US account holders.

**How much does it cost for Shopify sellers?**
Standard is $197 with 7-10 business day preparation. Express is $297 with 2-3 business day preparation.

**What tax form do Shopify sellers file?**
Most non-US Shopify sellers file Form 1040-NR and Schedule C. LLC owners also file Form 5472.

**Do I need an LLC to sell on Shopify?**
No, but many sellers form an LLC for liability protection. See our [ITIN + LLC formation service](/itin-llc-formation-service).
