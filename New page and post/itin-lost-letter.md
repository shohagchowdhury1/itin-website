## What Is Included in Every ITIN Lost Letter Package?

Both the $197 Standard and $297 Express packages include 4 core services. Our Certifying Acceptance Agent handles the entire replacement process.

### Complete W-7 Form Preparation

Form W-7 is the official IRS form for ITIN replacement. Errors on this form are the #1 reason for rejection. Our team completes every section correctly to request a new CP565 notice.

### Document Review Against 13 IRS-Accepted IDs

The IRS accepts 13 identity documents for ITIN replacements. Before we submit anything, we review every document against current IRS requirements.

### IRS Submission and Free Resubmission

We compile your complete replacement package and submit it to the IRS. Your W-7 form and supporting documents are sent together. If the IRS requests more information, we resubmit at no additional cost.

### No Passport Mailing Required

Working with an IRS-authorized Certifying Acceptance Agent (per IRS Publication 1915) means you do not mail your original passport to the IRS. We review and certify your identity documents. Your passport stays with you.

## ITIN Lost Letter FAQ (2026)

**What if I lost my ITIN letter?**
You can request a replacement CP565 notice by submitting a new Form W-7.

**Will my ITIN number change?**
No. You keep the same ITIN number. The IRS sends a new letter with your existing number.

**How much does a replacement letter cost?**
Standard is $197 with 7-10 business day preparation. Express is $297 with 2-3 business day preparation.

**Can I file taxes without the CP565 letter?**
Yes, if you know your ITIN number. But the letter is helpful for banks and employers.

**How do I request a replacement?**
Submit Form W-7 with your existing ITIN number and current identity documents.
