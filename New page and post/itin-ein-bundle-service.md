## What Is Included in Every ITIN + EIN Bundle Service Package?

Both the $197 Standard and $297 Express packages include 4 core services. Our Certifying Acceptance Agent handles the entire process.

### Complete W-7 and EIN Form Preparation

Form W-7 is the official IRS application for an ITIN. Form SS-4 is the official application for an EIN. Errors on either form cause rejections. Our team completes both forms based on your business structure and personal situation.

### Document Review Against 13 IRS-Accepted IDs

The IRS accepts 13 identity documents for ITIN applications. Before we submit anything, we review every document you provide against current IRS requirements. If something is missing, we tell you before it becomes a rejection.

### IRS Submission and Free Resubmission

We compile your complete bundle package and submit both applications to the IRS. Your W-7 form, SS-4 form, supporting documents, and tax return are sent together. If the IRS requests corrections, we resubmit at no additional cost.

### No Passport Mailing Required

Working with an IRS-authorized Certifying Acceptance Agent (per IRS Publication 1915) means you do not mail your original passport to the IRS. We review and certify your identity documents. Your passport stays with you.

## ITIN + EIN Bundle Service FAQ (2026)

**What is the ITIN + EIN bundle?**
It is a combined service where we prepare and submit both your ITIN application (Form W-7) and your EIN application (Form SS-4) at the same time.

**Do I need an ITIN to get an EIN?**
No. But if you are a non-US person, you often need both. The ITIN is for your personal taxes. The EIN is for your business.

**How much does the bundle cost?**
Standard is $197 with 7-10 business day preparation. Express is $297 with 2-3 business day preparation.

**Will I get my EIN before my ITIN?**
