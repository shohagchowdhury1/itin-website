## What Is Included in Every ITIN for Investment Accounts Package?

Both the $197 Standard and $297 Express packages include 4 core services. Our Certifying Acceptance Agent handles the entire process.

### Complete W-7 Form Preparation

Form W-7 is the official IRS application for an ITIN. Errors on this form are the #1 reason for ITIN rejections. Our team completes every section based on your situation as an investor, whether you trade stocks, bonds, or ETFs.

### Document Review Against 13 IRS-Accepted IDs

The IRS accepts 13 identity documents for ITIN applications. Investment account applicants typically use their home country passport. Before we submit anything, we review every document against current IRS requirements.

### IRS Submission and Free Resubmission

We compile your complete application package and submit it to the IRS. Your W-7 form, supporting documents, and tax return are sent together. If the IRS requests corrections, we resubmit at no additional cost.

### No Passport Mailing Required

Working with an IRS-authorized Certifying Acceptance Agent (per IRS Publication 1915) means you do not mail your original passport to the IRS. We review and certify your identity documents. Your passport stays with you.

## ITIN for Investment Accounts FAQ (2026)

**Can I open a US brokerage account with an ITIN?**
Some brokers accept ITINs. Interactive Brokers and some other firms allow non-resident accounts.

**Do I pay US taxes on investment income with an ITIN?**
Yes. Dividends and interest are subject to US withholding tax. Capital gains may be exempt for non-residents.

**How much does it cost to get an ITIN for investing?**
Standard is $197 with 7-10 business day preparation. Express is $297 with 2-3 business day preparation.

**Can I use Robinhood with an ITIN?**
Robinhood generally requires an SSN. Other brokers are more friendly to ITIN holders.

**Do I need to file a tax return for investment income?**
Yes. You file Form 1040-NR to report US investment income and claim treaty benefits.
