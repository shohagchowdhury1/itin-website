## What Is Included in Every ITIN Not Received Package?

Both the $197 Standard and $297 Express packages include 4 core services. Our Certifying Acceptance Agent handles the entire investigation.

### Status Investigation

We investigate why your ITIN letter has not arrived. Common causes include wrong mailing address, postal delays, or IRS processing issues.

### IRS Follow-Up

We contact the IRS ITIN Operation to check if your ITIN was issued and where it was mailed.

### Resolution and Free Re-Check

If your letter was lost, we help you request a replacement. If your application is still processing, we provide updates.

### No Passport Mailing Required

If a replacement application is needed, working with a CAA means you do not mail your original passport to the IRS.
