## What Is Included in Every ITIN Delayed Processing Package?

Both the $197 Standard and $297 Express packages include 4 core services. Our Certifying Acceptance Agent handles the entire delay resolution process.

### Status Investigation

We investigate the cause of your delay by reviewing your application date, documents, and any IRS correspondence. Common causes include missing documents, peak season backlogs, or address issues.

### IRS Follow-Up

We contact the IRS ITIN Operation on your behalf to check the status and identify any holds on your application.

### Resolution Support and Free Re-Check

If we find an issue, we help you fix it and resubmit if needed. We re-check your status at no additional cost.

### No Passport Mailing Required

If updated documents are needed, working with an IRS-authorized Certifying Acceptance Agent means you do not mail your original passport to the IRS.

## ITIN Delayed Processing FAQ (2026)

**Why is my ITIN taking so long?**
Common causes: peak season (Jan-Apr), missing documents, incorrect address, or IRS backlog.

**Can I speed up a delayed ITIN?**
We can investigate and follow up, but the IRS controls processing speed.

**How much does delay resolution cost?**
Standard is $197 with 7-10 business day investigation. Express is $297 with 2-3 business days.

**What if my application was lost?**
We help you resubmit with a new application if the IRS has no record.

**Can I check my status myself?**
Yes. Call 1-800-829-1040 domestically or 1-267-941-1000 internationally.
