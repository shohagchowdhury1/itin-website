## What Is Included in Every ITIN + LLC Formation Service Package?

Both the $197 Standard and $297 Express packages include 4 core services. Our Certifying Acceptance Agent handles the entire process.

### Complete LLC Formation and W-7 Form Preparation

We prepare your articles of organization for your LLC filing. We also complete Form W-7 for your ITIN and Form SS-4 for your EIN. Errors on any form cause delays. Our team completes all three correctly.

### Document Review Against 13 IRS-Accepted IDs

The IRS accepts 13 identity documents for ITIN applications. Before we submit anything, we review every document you provide against current IRS requirements. If something is missing, we tell you before it becomes a rejection.

### IRS and State Submission with Free Resubmission

We file your LLC with the state. We also submit your ITIN and EIN applications to the IRS. If any agency requests corrections, we resubmit at no additional cost.

### No Passport Mailing Required

Working with an IRS-authorized Certifying Acceptance Agent (per IRS Publication 1915) means you do not mail your original passport to the IRS. We review and certify your identity documents. Your passport stays with you.

**What is included in the ITIN + LLC formation service?**

Standard is $197 with 7-10 business day preparation. Express is $297 with 2-3 business day preparation. State filing fees are extra.

**Do I need an ITIN to form an LLC?**
No. But you need an ITIN to file personal US taxes as an LLC owner.

**Can I open a US bank account with just an LLC and EIN?**
Most banks also require an ITIN for the owner. We recommend getting all three.
