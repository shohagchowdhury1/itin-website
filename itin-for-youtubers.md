## What Is Included in Every ITIN for YouTubers Package?

Both the $197 Standard and $297 Express packages include 4 core services. Our Certifying Acceptance Agent handles the entire process.

### Complete W-7 Form Preparation

Form W-7 is the official IRS application for an ITIN. Errors on this form are the #1 reason for ITIN rejections. Our team completes every section based on your situation as a YouTuber, whether you earn from AdSense, sponsorships, or merchandise.

### Document Review Against 13 IRS-Accepted IDs

The IRS accepts 13 identity documents for ITIN applications. YouTubers typically use their home country passport. Before we submit anything, we review every document against current IRS requirements.

### IRS Submission and Free Resubmission

We compile your complete application package and submit it to the IRS. Your W-7 form, supporting documents, and tax return are sent together. If the IRS requests corrections, we resubmit at no additional cost.

### No Passport Mailing Required

Working with an IRS-authorized Certifying Acceptance Agent (per IRS Publication 1915) means you do not mail your original passport to the IRS. We review and certify your identity documents. Your passport stays with you.

## ITIN for YouTubers FAQ (2026)

**Do YouTubers need an ITIN?**
Yes. Google AdSense requires a tax ID for US-sourced ad revenue. Without one, they withhold up to 30%.

**Can I monetize YouTube without an ITIN?**
You can, but Google will withhold 30% of US ad revenue until you provide a valid tax ID.

**How much does it cost for YouTubers?**
Standard is $197 with 7-10 business day preparation. Express is $297 with 2-3 business day preparation.

**What tax form do YouTubers file?**
Most non-US YouTubers file Form 1040-NR and Schedule C. You report AdSense and sponsorship income.

**Can I claim tax treaty benefits as a YouTuber?**
Yes. Many countries have tax treaties that reduce withholding to 0%. An ITIN is required to claim them.
