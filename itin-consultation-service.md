## What Is Included in Every ITIN Consultation Service Package?

Both the $197 Standard and $297 Express packages include 4 core services. Our Certifying Acceptance Agent handles the entire process.

### Personal One-on-One Consultation

You get a 30-minute consultation with an ITIN specialist. We review your visa status, income sources, and tax obligations. We answer your questions and recommend the best application strategy for your situation.

### Complete W-7 Form Preparation

After the consultation, our team completes every section of Form W-7 based on your specific circumstances. Errors on this form are the #1 reason for ITIN rejections.

### Document Review Against 13 IRS-Accepted IDs

The IRS accepts 13 identity documents for ITIN applications. Before we submit anything, we review every document you provide against current IRS requirements. If something is missing, we tell you before it becomes a rejection.

### IRS Submission and Free Resubmission

We compile your complete application package and submit it to the IRS. Your W-7 form, supporting documents, and tax return are sent together. If the IRS requests corrections, we resubmit at no additional cost.
