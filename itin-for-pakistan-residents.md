## What Is Included in Every ITIN for Pakistan Residents Package?

Both the $197 Standard and $297 Express packages include 4 core services. Our Certifying Acceptance Agent handles the entire process remotely from Pakistan.

### Complete W-7 Form Preparation

Form W-7 is the official IRS application for an ITIN. Errors on this form are the #1 reason for ITIN rejections. Our team completes every section based on your situation as a Pakistan resident, whether you are a freelancer, Amazon seller, or investor.

### Document Review Against 13 IRS-Accepted IDs

The IRS accepts 13 identity documents for ITIN applications. Most Pakistan residents use their Pakistan passport. We also accept the CNIC (Computerized National Identity Card) as a secondary document. Before we submit anything, we review every document against current IRS requirements.

### IRS Submission and Free Resubmission

We compile your complete application package and submit it to the IRS. Your W-7 form, supporting documents, and tax return are sent together. If the IRS requests corrections, we resubmit at no additional cost.

### No Passport Mailing Required

Working with an IRS-authorized Certifying Acceptance Agent (per IRS Publication 1915) means you do not mail your original Pakistan passport to the IRS. We review and certify your identity documents. Your passport stays with you in Pakistan.
