Both the $197 Standard and $297 Express packages include 4 core services. Our Certifying Acceptance Agent handles the entire renewal process.

### Complete W-7 Form Preparation

Form W-7 is the official IRS form for ITIN renewal. Errors on this form are the #1 reason for rejection. Our team completes every section correctly to renew your expired ITIN.

### Expired ITIN Analysis

We check if your ITIN expired due to non-use or because it had middle digits 70-88. We determine the correct renewal path for your situation.

### IRS Submission and Free Resubmission

We compile your complete renewal package and submit it to the IRS. Your W-7 form and supporting documents are sent together. If the IRS requests corrections, we resubmit at no additional cost.

Working with an IRS-authorized Certifying Acceptance Agent (per IRS Publication 1915) means you do not mail your original passport to the IRS. We review and certify your identity documents. Your passport stays with you.

## ITIN Expired Issues FAQ (2026)

**How do I know if my ITIN expired?**
ITINs with middle digits 70-88 expired. ITINs not used on a tax return for 3 years also expire.

**Can I renew an expired ITIN?**
Yes. Submit Form W-7 with your expired ITIN number and current documents.

**How much does renewal cost?**
Standard is $197 with 7-10 business day preparation. Express is $297 with 2-3 business day preparation.

**What happens if I file taxes with an expired ITIN?**
The IRS may process your return but delay your refund. Renew before filing.

**Do I need a tax return to renew?**
No. Renewal does not require a tax return unless you are also filing taxes.
