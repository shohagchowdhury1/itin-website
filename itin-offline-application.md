## What Is Included in Every ITIN Offline Application Package?

Both the $197 Standard and $297 Express packages include 4 core services. Our Certifying Acceptance Agent handles the entire offline process.

### Complete W-7 Form Preparation

Form W-7 is the official IRS application for an ITIN. Errors on this form are the #1 reason for ITIN rejections. Our team completes every section based on your situation for offline submission.

### Document Handling and Packaging

We prepare your physical application package with all required forms and documents. We ensure everything is properly organized for IRS mail processing.

### IRS Mail Submission and Free Resubmission

We mail your complete application package to the IRS ITIN Operation in Austin, Texas. If the IRS requests corrections, we resubmit at no additional cost.

### No Passport Mailing Required

Working with an IRS-authorized Certifying Acceptance Agent (per IRS Publication 1915) means you do not mail your original passport to the IRS. We certify your documents and handle the mailing.

## ITIN Offline Application FAQ (2026)

**Can I apply for an ITIN offline?**
Yes. Our CAA service handles offline mail submissions to the IRS.

**Is offline application different from online?**
The preparation is online, but the IRS submission is by mail. The IRS does not accept fully electronic ITIN applications.

**How much does offline application cost?**
Standard is $197 with 7-10 business day preparation. Express is $297 with 2-3 business day preparation.

**Do I need to mail my passport for offline application?**
No. As your CAA, we certify your documents so your passport stays with you.

**Where does the IRS process offline applications?**
All ITIN applications are processed at the IRS ITIN Operation in Austin, Texas.
